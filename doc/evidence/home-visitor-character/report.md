## Read-only observation interface proposal

`VisitorStatus` prints one record with engine frame/world time, pawn/view-target identities, position/control rotation/velocity, movement mode/grounded/falling, capsule dimensions, CurrentFloor blocking/walkable/distance and actor/component identity, and a blocking capsule overlap query that ignores the visitor itself. `VisitorTrace <seconds>` explicitly samples at 0.2-second intervals for a finite duration clamped to 0–10 seconds (default 3); it also logs actual capsule blocking-hit events with actor/component, impact point/normal and initial penetration. Sampling is off by default, has no HUD surface and cannot advance or teleport. Recovery independently records threshold bounds and position before recovery, followed by the entry result.
